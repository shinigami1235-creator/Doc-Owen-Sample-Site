About

An eye surgeon who also
does aesthetic surgeryFPAPSHPI, FPAAS, FPAAAMMI, FPFCS, FAAPAM

Dr. Owen trained in ophthalmology at Manila Doctors Hospital and still sees eye patients today. She went on to train in aesthetic dermatology and cosmetic surgery, and now runs the aesthetic medicine department across the Dr. Chio clinics, sits on the board of the national academy of aesthetic surgery, and teaches other doctors in the Philippines and abroad.

Appointments

How to book

Consultations are by appointment. Message Dr. Owen with the treatment you want and she will tell you which of her clinics to come to and when.

Message Dr. Owen
  1. Send a messageSay what you want treated and which days suit you.
  2. She assigns the clinicDr. Owen holds clinic at more than one branch and will tell you which one to come to.
  3. ConsultationShe examines you and tells you what is possible, what it costs and what it will not fix.
  4. TreatmentBooked for a later date, or done the same day when it is a short procedure.
