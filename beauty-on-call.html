Services

What Dr. Owen treats

Every treatment is done or supervised by Dr. Owen herself.

01

Aesthetic surgery

Cosmetic surgery of the face and body. Dr. Owen trained in cosmetic surgery through the Philippine Academy of Aesthetic Surgery, and eyelid work is done by a doctor who trained first as an eye specialist.

02

Injectables and non-surgical

Fillers, biostimulators and skin treatments done in the clinic without surgery.

03

Regenerative medicine

Treatments built on regenerative medicine, the field she speaks on at conferences in Asia.

04

Hair

Consultation and treatment for thinning hair and hair loss.

05

Training for doctors

Talks, workshops and hands-on training in aesthetic medicine and surgery for other doctors.

The doctor

Dr. Owen Aguilar-Joven

Dr. Ma. Rowena C. Aguilar-Joven, MD is an ophthalmologist who trained in aesthetic dermatology and cosmetic surgery. She sits on the Board of Trustees of the Philippine Academy of Aesthetic Surgery and teaches other doctors at conferences in the Philippines and abroad.
